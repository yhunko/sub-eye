# Per-Type Add Flows Implementation Plan

Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. One conventional commit per task; every commit builds and leaves the tests it touches green.

**Goal:** Adding a subscription stops asking for a price before the app knows what is being added. The + menu offers one flow per type — Subscription, Free trial, One-time license, Installment or loan — and each flow asks only for its own terms, in the order the user thinks about them.

**Architecture:** `SubscriptionEditor` stays the one view for subscriptions and gains a `Flow` (`subscription`, `trial`, `license`). Every new flow is two steps: the existing brand step, then one terms page built for that flow from shared field blocks. Editing (`original != nil`) keeps today's single page. All flows save through one pure static builder, `SubscriptionEditor.value(_:firstPayment:offer:)`, which validates the form and turns it into the `Subscription` and `OfferInput?` that `Repository.save` already takes. The + menu becomes a keyed list, `AddKind`, whose cases carry their title, symbol, identifier, Pro flag and sheet. `InstallmentEditor` is untouched. `SubEyeCore` changes in one place only: the reminder planner stops sending a renewal reminder for the charge a trial reminder already announces (Task 2).

**Tech stack:** Swift 6, SwiftUI, XCTest (`SubEyeCoreTests`, `SubEyeTests`, `SubEyeUITests`), XcodeBuildMCP.

## Decisions (made with Yehor)

- The + menu (`AddSubscriptionButton`) chooses the type; the subscription editor's offer chooser on step 3 ("How you pay": Regular / Free trial / Intro discount / One-time license) goes away.
- **Subscription:** brand, then one page: price, cadence, category, first payment date, an optional Pro "Starts at a lower price" section (offer price and end date), the existing advanced section (auto-paid, notes), and the plan preview when the offer is on.
- **Free trial (Pro):** brand, then the trial end date first, then the price after the trial and the cadence.
- **One-time license (Pro):** brand, then license price and purchase date, then the updates renewal price with the included-updates window shown. No reused price field.
- **Installment or loan:** unchanged.
- For free users Free trial and One-time license stay in the menu with a lock and open the paywall.
- The menu keeps `addSubscription`; items are `newSubscription`, `newTrial`, `newLicense`, `newInstallment`.
- Shared in every flow: brand search and logo style, the counterpart banner, dirty tracking with the discard menu and `interactiveDismissDisabled`, the one-time reminders offer after creation, Pro gating of category, keyboard and focus handling, the sticky primary button.
- Editing keeps one page showing the subscription's terms, with no offer chooser.
- Core was meant to stay unchanged, with each flow storing what today's editor stores for that offer kind. **This plan departs from that in two places, both listed below for approval:** the free trial is stored anchored on its end (decision 1), and core changes in one place, the reminder planner (decision 2, Task 2, with a test that fails without it). Every other flow stores what today's editor stores. Where a pricing trap needed a product choice the design did not cover, the plan picks what the user's bank actually charges (decisions 1–6 below).

## Decisions this plan makes (check before approving)

### Pricing traps

1. **A trial is anchored on its end: the trial end is the first paid charge.** Core charges on `paymentDate`'s cycle and phases only price those charges (`Projection.payments`). Today's editor anchors a trial on "First payment" (default today), so a 7-day monthly trial stores a `$0.00` payment today, sends a `$0` renewal reminder, and charges for the first time a month after it started — while its own preview says "Then monthly · {end}". A bank charges on the day the trial ends and every cycle from there. So the trial flow asks one date, "Trial ends" (footnote: "The first payment is due that day."), and saves `paymentDate = trialEnd` with `OfferInput(promoCost: "0", standardCost: price, currency, endDate: trialEnd)`, `deferred` false. This is the one record that differs from today's editor (same `OfferInput`, different `paymentDate`); the app's own `trialEnding` scenario already ends its trial on a payment day.
2. **One reminder for the charge that ends a trial (core change, Task 2).** With the anchor on the end, a Pro user with trial and renewal reminders on gets "{name} trial ends tomorrow — $11.99 starts then unless you cancel" and "{name} renews tomorrow — $11.99 will be charged" the same morning, because `ReminderPlanner.plan` keys groups on kind. The bank charges once, so `plan` drops the renewal reminder for a payment dated on the effective trial's end at every lead the trial reminders also use. Renewal leads the trial does not use (e.g. 7 days) and users with trial reminders off (all free users: `effective(pro:)` turns trials off) keep the renewal reminder, so nobody loses the warning before the charge. A `ReminderTests` test fails without it. This already happens today for any trial that ends on a payment day (the `trialEnding` scenario); the new flow makes it every trial. The rule is in the planner, so it also applies to a free trial started from Manage pricing whose end falls on a payment day; the commit body says so.
3. **Trial start is not asked; the trial must end after now.** Core starts the trial phase at the save instant (the detail's "Trial started" shows the save day) and rejects an end that is not after now (`offerEndsAt`/`boundary`). The trial flow does not ask a start date core would ignore. The "Trial ends" picker's range starts at `Day.shift(Day.floor(Date()), days: 1)` — the first UTC midnight after now, exactly what core accepts — instead of the device's tomorrow, and the builder also rejects an end that is not after `now` (the range is computed when the page renders, so a page left open past midnight could hold a stale date). Save is disabled with a footnote instead of throwing. West of UTC this means a trial ending tomorrow cannot be entered late in the day (Known limits); core is not changed for it. An already-converted trial is added as a Subscription.
4. **The kind is derived from the price, so the builder enforces each flow's rules.** Core makes a `.trial` phase whenever `promoCost` is 0 and checks neither promo < standard, renewal > 0, nor the end against the first payment. A pure `SubscriptionEditor.rejection(_:firstPayment:offer:now:zone:) -> String?` returns the footnote key for the first broken rule; `value(...)` throws `DomainError.invalidField` with that text, and the page disables Save and shows the footnote above it. The rules:
   - intro price 0 → `form_introFree` ("A free start is a free trial — add it from + → Free trial."); today's editor saved such an intro as a trial;
   - intro price not below the regular price → `form_introTooHigh`;
   - intro end on or before the first payment, or not after the first charge on or after today → `form_introEndEarly` (an intro that discounts no charge would still set `sub.cost` to the promo; the page also re-clamps `offerEnd` when the first payment moves while the intro is on);
   - a trial or intro end not after `now` → `form_endTooSoon`;
   - a trial price, license price or renewal of 0 → `form_priceAboveZero`.

   A plain subscription may still cost 0, as today. An empty or unparsable price disables Save without a footnote, as today.
5. **An intro keeps its end date; the preview shows the first full-price charge.** Yehor's design asks for an end date. Core prices each charge by the phase on its day, so with an end between payments the last discounted charge is the one before the end and the first full-price charge is the next payment day — how a bank applies an intro that ends mid-cycle. The `introEnds` calendar event stays on the end date (core). Only the preview was wrong: its "Then {cadence}" row moves from the end date to the first charge on or after it (`Recurrence.occurrence` at `nextIndex(onOrAfter: end)`). Counting discounted payments (`OfferInput.payments`, as Manage pricing does) is not adopted: it counts from the first payment on or after today, not from the anchor, and Yehor asked for a date.
6. **A license maps every field explicitly.** The date is the purchase date. `Pricing.license` overwrites `Subscription.cost` with the renewal, and the license price lives only in the `.license` phase. The cadence is the updates window (one cycle). The flow passes `value.cost = Display.amount(renewal)` (it must parse: `LegacyMigration.validate` runs before the offer rewrites it), `OfferInput(promoCost: Display.amount(licensePrice), standardCost: value.cost, currency, license: true)`, and `paymentDate = purchase`. A purchase older than one cycle applies the renewal at save and a future purchase starts the license phase now, both as today. The renewal stays required (> 0) although the brief says "optional": core has no renewal-free license, and a `0.00` renewal would put a free "updates end" decision on every cycle forever (`Projection.events`). The renewal's footnote (`form_renewalHint`) says what the field is without contradicting that it is required: "What another period of updates would cost. Renewing is up to you." A new license defaults to yearly (a year of updates is the common case and what the store copy describes); the cadence is labelled "Updates period", visibly and for VoiceOver.
7. **Editing cannot add or change an offer, and stays as it is.** `Repository.save` throws `illegalAction` for an offer on an existing record, and an edit that moves a trial's or intro's `paymentDate` does not move its phases (only licenses realign). The edit page keeps today's single page with no offer controls. Type switching of an existing row is not offered, so nothing routes through `PricingView`'s `startOffer`, which deletes a license's phases.
8. **Pro gating stays in the UI.** Core has no Pro check, as today for every Pro feature. The gates are the menu items (`.paywall`), the intro card (local paywall sheet) and the category row. A locked menu item shows "Pro" (`paywall_badge`) as its subtitle (a second `Text` in the label, which UIKit maps to `UIMenuElement.subtitle`, visible and spoken; a menu item has no accessibility value) next to the lock image. The locked intro card carries the accessibility value "Pro"; the unlocked one has the `.isToggle` trait on iOS 17+ and `.isSelected` when on. UI tests assert the subtitle is there for a free user and absent for Pro.

### Shape

9. **`draft.cost` is always the recurring price.** Subscription: the price. Intro: the regular price. Trial: the price after the trial. License: the renewal. The first or one-off price (intro, license) lives in `offerPrice`. This removes the `EditorView.swift:254` workaround.
10. **The menu is `AddKind`, separate from the editor's `Flow`.** `AddKind` has `subscription`, `trial`, `license`, `installment`; each case's title key (`subs_newTrial` …), identifier (`newTrial` …), symbol, `pro` flag and `SheetRoute` are written literally in a `switch`, so a grep for a key finds its reader. `SubscriptionEditor.Flow` has only the three subscription flows, so the editor never receives `installment`. A new record type is one `AddKind` case; a new subscription flow is one `AddKind` case plus its `Flow` case and page. A locked item shows `lock` instead of its symbol (a menu row has one image) and sets `sheet = .paywall`.
11. **`SheetRoute.editor(Subscription?, SubscriptionEditor.Flow = .subscription)`**, id `case .editor(let sub, let flow): "edit:" + (sub?.id ?? "new-" + flow.rawValue)`; the `MainShell` arm becomes `case .editor(let original, let flow)`. The default keeps `DetailView`'s edit and both empty states unchanged; distinct ids keep `.sheet(item:)` presenting a fresh editor per flow.
12. **Empty states keep their buttons; trial and license are not added.** `EmptySubscriptions` (`Components.swift:301`) keeps "Add subscription" and "Add installment or loan"; the Subscriptions segment's own empty state (`SubscriptionsView.swift:107`, shown when only plans exist) has one button and keeps it. The empty state is a free user's first screen, where both would be locks, and the + menu with all four sits in the same navigation bar.
13. **The intro section is the offer row it replaces**, used as a toggle: the same card (`tag` icon, title, hint, `lock` for free users, a check when on), identifier `introOffer`. A card button rather than a `Toggle`, because XCUITest taps buttons reliably and the offer rows already work this way.
14. **Field order.** Subscription: brand card; name, price, category; cadence; first payment; intro; advanced; preview. Trial: brand card; trial end; name, price after the trial, category; cadence; advanced; preview. License: brand card; name, license price, category; purchase date; updates period; updates until and renewal (footnote); advanced; preview. Name sits with the first price, as today, so the keyboard's Next goes name → that price: one per-page `nameNext: Field` drives both the name field's `.onSubmit` and the toolbar's `nextEditorField` (both hard-code `.price` today). Every price field gets a VoiceOver label equal to its visible title (today's `offerPrice` has none), and every focusable block keeps both `.focused(...)` and `.id(Field.x)`, which `KeyboardAwareScrollView` scrolls to.
15. **`dirty`** = draft, date or logo style changed, `intro` on, `offerPrice` typed, or `offerEnd` moved off its default. Today's `offer != "none"` term would make a trial page dirty before anything is typed.
16. **Order of tasks.** Trial and license move out of the chooser one task at a time before the Subscription flow removes it, so no commit loses a way to add an offer. Strings a task orphans are deleted in that task, after `/usr/bin/grep` finds no reader (the chooser builds keys as `"form_offer" + mode.capitalized`, which a literal grep misses).

## What each flow saves

`SubscriptionEditor.value(_ draft: Subscription, firstPayment: Date, offer: Offer, now: Date, zone: TimeZone = .current) throws -> (subscription: Subscription, offer: OfferInput?)` (it calls `rejection(...)` first; `now`/`zone` give today for the first-charge rule), with `enum Offer: Equatable { case none, intro(price: String, ends: Date), trial(ends: Date), license(price: String) }`. Common to all: `name` trimmed; `cost = Display.amount(draft.cost)`; `currency`, `every`, `period`, `categoryId`, `brandDomain`, `autoPaid`, `notes` from the form; `paymentDate = Day.iso(Day.floor(firstPayment))` except for a trial. Then `repository.save(value, expected: original, offer:, rates:, now:)` as today.

| Flow | `paymentDate` | `cost` passed | `OfferInput` | Stored after `Repository.save` | Rejected by the builder | Pinned by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription | first payment | price | `nil` | `sub` at the price, no phases | — | `testRegularSubscriptionStoresNoPhases` |
| Subscription + lower start | first payment | regular price | `promoCost: offerPrice, standardCost: cost, currency, endDate: floor(offerEnd)` | `sub.cost` = offer price; `intro` phase from now to the end, applied; `standard` from the end, unapplied | offer price 0 or ≥ regular; end ≤ first payment, or not after the first charge from today, or not after now | `testIntroStartsAtTheOfferPriceUntilItsEnd`, `testIntroMustStartLower` |
| Free trial | **trial end** | price after the trial | `promoCost: "0", standardCost: cost, currency, endDate: floor(trialEnd)` | `sub.cost` `0.00`; stored `paymentDate` equals the stored trial `endsAt`; `trial` phase from now to the end, applied; `standard` from the end; first projected payment on the end day at the full price; nothing charged before it | price 0; end not after now | `testTrialIsFreeUntilItsEndAndChargedThatDay` |
| One-time license | purchase date | renewal | `promoCost: licensePrice, standardCost: cost, currency, license: true` | `sub.cost` = renewal; `license` phase from min(purchase, now) to one cycle later, applied; `standard` at the renewal from the window end (applied when already past) | license or renewal 0 | `testLicenseChargesThePurchaseOnceAndRenewsUpdates`, `testLicenseBoughtTodayChargesItsPriceToday` |
| Edit | as edited | as edited | `nil` | as today | — | existing UI tests |

Plus `testPreviewShowsTheFirstFullPriceCharge` (decision 5) on a pure `SubscriptionEditor.plan(...)` that returns the preview's dates and amounts. All live in `SubEyeTests/SubscriptionEditorTests.swift`.

## Known limits

- West of UTC, from local UTC-midnight on (16:00 in Los Angeles, 19:00 in New York), the earliest trial or intro end is the user's day after tomorrow: core needs the end's UTC midnight to be after the save instant. A trial ending tomorrow cannot be added then. Today's editor throws on Save in the same case; core is not changed for it.
- Editing a trial or an intro and moving its first payment date leaves the offer's end where it was (core realigns only licenses), as today.
- Editing a license still labels its renewal "Price" and its purchase date "First payment", as today.
- Until the detail is opened and settles the phases, a trial that has ended still counts at `0.00` in the monthly total (`row.amount` reads `Subscription.cost`), as today.
- An intro has no reminder of its own; its first full-price charge gets the ordinary renewal reminder, as today.

## Explicitly not doing

Changing the edit page; switching an existing subscription between types; a renewal-free license; a trial start date; intros counted in payments or deferred (`OfferInput.payments`, `deferred`); trial or license buttons in the empty states; Manage pricing (`PricingView`); any core change beyond Task 2.
